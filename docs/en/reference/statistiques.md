Statistics can come from several systems. Player level, jobs, pets, weapon, fruit and titan do not necessarily use the same formulas.

## Comparison method

1. Note level and rarity.
2. Disable unnecessary boosts.
3. Compare one piece of equipment at a time.
4. Capture the panel before and after.

The **Stats** plugin centralises some values, while **PlayerLevel**, **DevilishFruit** and **DevilTitan** calculate their own progression. An internal formula should not be presented as guaranteed in-game damage without a test.
