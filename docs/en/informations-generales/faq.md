## Where can I find current information?

Official announcements and staff take priority over the wiki. If a value differs between this page and the game, report it with a screenshot and the date of the observation.

## Why is a menu empty?

A system may be limited by your progression, rank, permission, area or an active event. Check the displayed message, then reopen the menu after reconnecting.

## How do I claim a pending reward?

For jobs, the configuration message provides **/jobs claim** when rewards are waiting. Other systems use their own reward menu: do not mix job, vote, pass and event rewards.

## Can I share an account?

No. Follow the server rules and never share a password, token or private information, even with someone claiming to be staff.

## How do I request a correction?

Open the relevant page, choose **Suggest an edit**, then describe exactly what should change. See [Contribute to the wiki](/en/contribuer/).
