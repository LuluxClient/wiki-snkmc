The server has a voting flow with associated rewards. The reference environment contains **Voty**, **BetterVotePlugin** and **Rewards**, but links and amounts may be changed by staff.

## Good habits

1. Use only links provided by the server or its official channels.
2. Check that your player name is correct before confirming a vote.
3. Wait briefly after voting if the reward is delivered automatically.
4. Check the rewards menu before claiming more than once.

If a vote is counted but a reward is missing, note the voting site, time and player name, then contact support. Never share your account credentials.
