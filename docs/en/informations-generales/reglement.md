This page is the editorial structure for the SnkMC rules. The detailed official rules must be validated and completed by staff before final publication.

## Respect and behaviour

- Treat players, teams and staff with respect.
- Do not use harassment, threats, discrimination or abusive language.
- Do not attempt to bypass a restriction or a punishment.

## Cheating and exploits

- Do not use software or behaviour that creates an unfair advantage.
- Do not intentionally reproduce a bug; report it to staff.
- Do not abuse duplication, economy exploits or permission defects.

## Trading and economy

- Check the item, quantity and price before confirming a trade.
- Do not present an external transaction as an official server sale.
- Contact staff about fraud, abnormal loss or a rollback.

:::caution[Validation required]
This page does not replace the official rules announcement. Sanctions and exceptions must be added only after validation by the SnkMC staff team.
:::
