## Before your first connection

- Use a Minecraft installation compatible with the version announced by staff.
- Make sure your account has multiplayer access.
- Join the official channels to find the current address, maintenance notices and prerequisites.
- Keep your in-game name and linked-service account consistent.

The connection address, exact version and any required mods should be added here after validation by the SnkMC team. They are deliberately not guessed in this first edition.

## First loading

1. Join the server and read the welcome messages.
2. Open the main menu and locate camp, adventure, jobs and rewards.
3. Follow the tutorial before spending your first resources.
4. If a menu is unavailable, reconnect and check the maintenance announcements.
