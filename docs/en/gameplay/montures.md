The **Mounters** system organises mounts by rarity. Its menu separates the list, management, storage and food panels.

## Collection found in the reference configuration

| Rarity | Mounts |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Brown, cream, isabelle and light-brown horses |
| Rare | Chestnut, brown-piebald, piebald and mouse horses |
| Epic | Fire, lightning, water and wind elemental horses |
| Legendary | Kelpie, Kirin, Pegasus and Unicorn |
| Mythic | Giant Horse and Sleipnir |

Equip a mount from its menu and check its food before a long trip. Stats and speed should be completed from the active in-game menus if they change with progression.
