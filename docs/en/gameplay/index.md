SnkMC gameplay follows a loop: earn experience and resources, improve your equipment, explore a harder area, then invest the rewards into your character and camp.

## Progression axes

| Axis | What it provides |
| --- | --- |
| Player level | General progression, ascensions and boosts |
| Ranks | Goals and recognition milestones |
| Jobs | Experience, money and points |
| Equipment | Damage, stats and adventure access |
| Camp | Storage, production, permissions and group play |
| Collection | Pets, mounts, cosmetics, scrolls and rewards |

These pages describe player-facing systems. Production and economy values may be rebalanced.
