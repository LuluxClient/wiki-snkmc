Before leaving, check four elements: player level, rank, weapon progression and pet bonuses. Add a mount if travel matters and keep consumables accessible.

Stats may be displayed through **Stats**, **PlayerLevel**, **DevilishFruit** and **DevilTitan**. A value shown by an item may depend on level, rarity, ascension or an active boost.

:::tip
To compare two equipment choices, change one element at a time and record the result. A comparison made with several active boosts is difficult to interpret.
:::
