## Experience

The reference configuration uses this experience target:

**Required XP = 250 + 75 × level²**

The configured maximum player level is **200**. Activities, jobs, adventure and some pets can contribute to progression depending on active boosts.

## Ascensions

Ascension milestones are configured at levels **10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100**. Each milestone may require items, currency or a combination of resources.

Before confirming an ascension:

1. open the level panel;
2. note every required resource;
3. leave room in your inventory;
4. check that the new progress was saved.
