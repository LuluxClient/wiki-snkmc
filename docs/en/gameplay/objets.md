The SnkMC catalogue is split between gameplay items, item models and adventure items. Inventory families include weapons, titan materials, medals, seeds, farm resources and area rewards.

## Reading an item panel

- The name and icon identify the item.
- Rarity indicates its collection level.
- Lore may describe use, level or source.
- A boss, dungeon or farm item is often intended for an ascension.

Internal names are not always the names players see. For a contribution, use the in-game name and the menu path rather than a technical identifier.
