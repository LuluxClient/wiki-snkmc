Fishing and farming feed several progression loops: jobs, rewards, ascensions and orders. Adventure areas may provide specific resources that are more valuable than starting resources.

## Production advice

- Keep common materials for early ascensions.
- Sort resources by area to remember where they came from.
- Check whether a quest asks for a quality, quantity or specific area.
- Avoid selling a rare resource before checking ascension costs.

The reference environment contains **Farmers** and **MoreFish**. Loot tables should be added with a screenshot or validated source to remain reliable.
