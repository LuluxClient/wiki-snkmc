The **PassEvolution** system groups quest categories and evolution milestones. A quest may request a general activity, a resource, an area or a specific fight.

## Progress efficiently

- Open the pass at the start of a session and find nearby objectives.
- Group quests that use the same area.
- Claim each milestone as soon as it unlocks.
- Note objectives requiring a boss or dungeon so you can prepare equipment.

Exact categories and rewards may be renewed. A season-specific page can be added without changing this structure.
