Pets are organised by rarity and can contribute to progression through boosts. The reference environment contains characters and creatures connected to the titan areas.

## Collection examples

- Common: Abnormal Titan, Connie, Corrupted Soldier, Hitch, Nanaba, Nifa, Oluo and Rico.
- Rare: Jean, Kenny, Connie’s Mother, Reiner, Royal Guard and Sasha.

Rarity is not automatically the best bonus for every activity. Read the pet details and compare the bonus with your objective: level, money, job, adventure or collection.
