## Available jobs

The reference configuration automatically joins these jobs:

| Job | Typical activity |
| --- | --- |
| Farmer | Crops and harvesting |
| Miner | Mined blocks and ores |
| Fisherman | Fish and special catches |
| Lumberjack | Wood and trees |
| Explorer | Travel and discovery |
| Hunter | Creatures and combat |

Each job provides experience, money and points. Boosts can affect shop, reward, adventure and pet gains separately.

## Pending rewards

The system message provides **/jobs claim** to collect pending job rewards. Leave room in your inventory: a reward should not be considered lost until its pending queue has been checked.

The reference limits are 100,000 experience, 100,000 income and 100 points. They are configuration safeguards and may change.
