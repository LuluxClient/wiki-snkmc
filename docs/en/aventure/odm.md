The item and combat systems include mobility equipment inspired by reconnaissance gear. The configuration exposes jump, sprint, attack and movement skills for titan-related systems.

## Usage advice

- Keep consumables and repair resources ready if the menu provides them.
- Test movement in a safe area before entering a dungeon.
- Check that weapon and mobility equipment match your combat style.
- Do not confuse movement speed, attack range and damage.

Read exact stats on the in-game item: they may vary with level, rarity or ascension.
