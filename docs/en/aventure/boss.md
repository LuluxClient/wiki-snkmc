## Bosses found in the menus

Adventure menus list the Small Abnormal Titan, Connie’s Mother, Kenny Ackerman, the Female Titan, the Armored Titan, the Attack Titan and the Legendary Boar.

## Preparation

- Read the boss panel and its area.
- Check your equipment’s level and resistance.
- Leave room for materials.
- If the fight is shared, decide who receives what before starting.

Ascensions use separate material categories: boss, dungeon and farm. A boss material is not interchangeable with a crop just because both have a similar rarity.
