Adventure weapons gain experience and use level-based damage formulas. The observed rarities are common, rare, epic and legendary.

## Weapons found in the reference configuration

- Common: Garrison Blade, Explorer Blade, Mitras Blade, Connie Blade and Kenny Blade.
- Rare: Annie Blade, Reiner Blade and Shiganshina Blade.
- Epic: Sasha Blade, Reiss Blade, plus Battalion, Reconnaissance and Forest variants.
- Legendary: Liberty Blade, Camouflage Blade and Master Explorer Blade.

Wood, stone, iron, gold, diamond and netherite blades act as basic models. Evolvable weapons consume experience items and use ascension milestones.

## Level a weapon

Use compatible experience items, watch the displayed level and prepare the next milestone cost. Ascensions may request boss, dungeon and farm materials from a specific area.
