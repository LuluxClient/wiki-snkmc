The adventure menu groups teleports to regions and fortresses. Access may depend on level, permission, cost or an active event.

## Before leaving

- Check your inventory and food.
- Note the area that contains the material you need.
- Prepare a route back to your camp.
- Do not confuse an adventure area with an instanced dungeon.

A mount can make travel easier, but it does not replace a suitable weapon. If a teleport disappears, check the menu requirements first.
