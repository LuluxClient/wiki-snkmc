Adventure is organised around regions, creatures, bosses and fortresses. It provides the materials needed for weapon, fruit and titan ascensions.

## Expedition loop

1. Prepare your weapon and consumables.
2. Check the level and rarity of the required resources.
3. Travel to an area suitable for your progression.
4. Defeat its creatures and boss.
5. Bring back boss, dungeon and farm materials.
6. Use them for an ascension or order.
