## Starting checklist

- Open the main menu and locate the progression tabs.
- Create or join a camp.
- Check your player level, rank and jobs.
- Leave inventory space for rewards.
- Try an adventure area only after preparing a weapon.
- Note the resources required for your next ascension.

## Learn these systems first

Player level drives general progression. Jobs provide experience, money and points. Your camp becomes a home for storage and production. Adventure areas provide boss, dungeon and farm materials used by ascensions.

:::tip[Useful habit]
When a menu shows a price, limit or requirement, take a screenshot before closing it. This makes a report much easier if the value looks incorrect.
:::
