## Main sources

- Jobs provide money, experience and points.
- Adventure areas provide boss, dungeon and farm materials.
- Votes and rewards provide variable gains.
- Shops and player sales let you exchange resources.
- Orders and events may request specific items.

## Spend with a plan

Keep a reserve for ascensions. An item or fruit may require money and several material categories at a given level. Compare the cost of an upgrade with the benefit it gives your play style.

Shop prices are dynamic. The player-shop configuration accepts values from 1 to 99,999,999,999 in the reference environment; that limit does not mean a high price is sensible.
