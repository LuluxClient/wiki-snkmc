SnkMC uses several components to display progression: **EpicHUD**, **BoardMaster**, **Hologrammer**, **SquidMenus** and **SquidCore**. Depending on the area and your progression, information can appear in a HUD, scoreboard, hologram or interactive menu.

## Reading a panel

- The level and experience bar show progression.
- Rarity distinguishes many items, pets, mounts and titans.
- Cost lines list required resources.
- A missing permission or requirement is different from a missing item.
