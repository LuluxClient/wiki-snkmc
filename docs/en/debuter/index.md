## Recommended path

1. Read [Join SnkMC](/en/informations-generales/rejoindre/).
2. Follow [the first steps](/en/debuter/premiers-pas/) and create your camp.
3. Start the six automatically joined jobs: farmer, miner, fisherman, lumberjack, explorer and hunter.
4. Raise your player level and earn your first rank.
5. Equip a weapon, pet or mount before entering adventure areas.

Do not spend every resource immediately: ascensions, camp upgrades and equipment use different materials.
