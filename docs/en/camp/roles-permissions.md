## Configured roles

The configuration identifies **Guest**, **Coop**, **Member**, **Moderator**, **Admin** and **Owner** roles. Coop is described as a temporary member.

Permissions can cover animals, monsters, chests, bank, placing, breaking, interacting and flying. Grant the lowest role that is sufficient for the task.

## Good habits

- Do not invite an unknown player with a high role.
- Remove temporary access after a building task.
- Check the member list before a sale or disband.
- Test a permission in an area without valuable items.
