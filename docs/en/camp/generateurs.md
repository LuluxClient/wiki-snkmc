The starting configuration observes these rates:

| Block | Starting rate |
| --- | ---: |
| Stone family | 70% cobblestone, 20% stone, 1% andesite, 1% granite, 1% diorite |
| Ores | 5% coal ore, 2% iron ore |

These percentages belong to the starting generator. Upgrades, prestige or another generator may use a different table.
