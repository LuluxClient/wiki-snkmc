Your camp is a group space: members, permissions, bank, generators, upgrades, warps and subareas all meet here.

## Main panels

- **Camp**: create, join and manage the island.
- **Treasure**: view value and treasure seals.
- **Members**: invite, remove and assign roles.
- **Settings**: enable or disable island rules.
- **Generators**: view production and upgrades.
- **Warps**: publish teleport points.
