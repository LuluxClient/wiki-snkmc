The camp panel references quests, a regiment leaderboard, military reserves, warps and upgrades. These features help compare camps and organise group progression.

## Suggested organisation

- Appoint a bank manager.
- Keep a list of shared resources.
- Define roles before a competitive activity.
- Take a screenshot of the leaderboard during an event.

War rules, rewards and seasons must be added only from an official announcement.
