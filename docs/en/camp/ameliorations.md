Upgrades turn resources into lasting convenience: border, members, warps, generators, block limits, entity limits and island settings.

## Starting limits found in the configuration

- 32 hoppers.
- 10 minecarts.
- 16 hopper minecarts.

Prestige may change rewards, generators or access to new milestones. Read the next-level preview before confirming because a camp upgrade can consume a resource shared with other systems.
