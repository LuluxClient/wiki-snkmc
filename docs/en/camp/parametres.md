Observed settings include:

- always day and always night;
- always rain and always sun;
- crop and tree growth;
- crop trampling;
- egg laying;
- lava and water flow;
- natural monster and animal spawning;
- mob and animal spawns from spawners;
- explosions.

Each setting has a name and description in the menu. Check the effect on members and farms before disabling one.
