The wiki evolves through pull requests. A useful contribution is small, verifiable and supported by context.

For a quick fix, select **Edit on GitHub** at the bottom of an article. For a live preview and images, open the [local visual editor](/editor/) after cloning your fork with GitHub Desktop. Save to your folder, then use **Commit**, **Push origin** and **Create Pull Request** in GitHub Desktop. Staff review the proposal before publication; no terminal commands or Cloudflare account are needed.

## A good proposal includes

- the page name;
- the detail to correct;
- the command or menu used;
- the date and observed version;
- a screenshot without private data;
- the affected language, French, English or both.

Configuration files help prepare content, but they do not replace an in-game validation for a price, loot table or active rule.
